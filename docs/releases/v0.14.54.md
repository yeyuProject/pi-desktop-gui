# Pi Desktop v0.14.54

- Remove the fixed 25-continuation Goal limit. Require a completion audit and distinguish paused, blocked, budget-limited and provider-quota states.
- Exclude cache hits from Goal usage, retain billed usage and estimated cost in details, and count only active execution time. Preserve legacy totals and evidence with a clear accounting label.
- Let current work wrap up after reaching a usage limit; stop if the model starts more than three additional requests. Adjust the limit and resume without losing accumulated usage.
- Edit objectives with `/goal edit` or the status bar, reject stale tool revisions, maintain up to twelve plan steps, and show the current step or elapsed time.
- Confirm replacement of unfinished Goals, discard superseded internal instructions at each model request, and use the objective for new conversation titles.
- Keep compact controls, eight-second completion collapse, legacy result navigation, read-only approval behavior, and explicit resume after restarting.

## 中文

- Goal 取消固定 25 次续跑限制，完成前逐项审计；暂停、受阻、达到上限和服务商额度不足分别显示。
- Goal 用量不计缓存命中，详情保留计费用量和估算费用，用时只累计执行期间。旧记录保留原用量与证据，并标明统计口径差异。
- 达到上限先完成当前工具并收尾；超过 3 次额外请求会中止。可以调整上限后继续，保留累计用量。
- 支持 `/goal edit` 和状态条编辑、过期版本校验、最多 12 项计划，状态条展示当前步骤或累计用时。
- 替换未完成目标前确认；每次模型请求排除过期内部提示，防止新旧目标串用；新会话标题取目标首行。
- 保留单行布局、完成后 8 秒收起、旧工具卡定位、只读模式，以及重启后手动恢复。
