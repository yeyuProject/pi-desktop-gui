# Pi Desktop v0.14.85

## Sidebar views

- Use the sidebar view menu next to Search to group sessions by project, date, or status, or show them in one list. Sort by last update, creation time, or name.
- Create sections to keep related sessions together. Drag a session into a section or use its menu, including while Pi is working or waiting for approval. Its project and working directory stay the same.
- Show pinned sessions in one group, toggle older-session folding or team members, and expand or collapse all groups. Your view and sections are saved when you restart Pi Desktop.

## 中文

### 侧栏显示方式

- 点击搜索按钮旁的「侧栏显示方式」，可以按项目、时间或状态分组，也可以不分组。会话可以按最近更新、创建时间或名称排序。
- 新建分区，把相关会话拖进去，或从会话菜单放入。Pi 正在运行或等待确认时也可以操作，会话仍使用原来的项目和工作目录。
- 可以集中显示置顶会话、切换旧会话折叠和团队成员显示，也可以全部展开或收起。重启后会保留显示方式和分区归属。
