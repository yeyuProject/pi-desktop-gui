# Pi Desktop v0.14.58

- Show ordinary task plans above the composer, with the current step, an expandable checklist and accessible status labels. The model decides when a task needs a plan; simple questions keep the existing layout.
- Keep plans tied to their conversation branch, including offline browsing and restarts. New tasks clear old plans; mid-run instructions preserve them. Closing a plan only hides it until the next model update.
- Minimize completed plans to a dot after eight idle seconds. Unfinished runs keep the remaining-step count visible and stop showing stale steps as running.
- Keep Goal plans and continuation behavior unchanged. Plan updates require no workspace approval and stay out of tool cards and in-session search.
- Support Pi Classic, Codex and Claude in light and dark modes, including long plans, font scaling and contrast checks.

## 中文

- 普通任务新增输入框上方的计划进度条，显示当前步骤，可展开完整清单，状态支持读屏。是否列计划由模型判断，简单问答保持原有界面。
- 计划随会话分支保存，离线查看与重启后也能恢复。新任务清除旧计划，运行中的补充消息保留计划；关闭只隐藏当前显示，模型下次更新时重新出现。
- 全部完成的计划在空闲八秒后收起为圆点。未完成时显示剩余步数，已经结束的步骤不再误示为执行中。
- Goal 的计划显示和续跑行为保持不变。更新计划无需工作区审批，也不会生成对话工具卡片或干扰会话内查找。
- 支持经典、Codex、Claude 三套风格的浅深色，覆盖长计划、字号缩放和文字对比度验收。
