# Pi Desktop v0.14.61

- Pi can ask up to three structured questions and continue the same turn after you answer, keeping the current task plan intact.
- Answer above the composer with single or multiple choices, recommendations, custom text and additional notes. Unsubmitted choices survive switching chats; question cards remain searchable and copyable in history.
- Plain text sent from the composer answers the first question and skips the remaining ones. Drafts containing attachments, images, snippets or quotes stay untouched until the questions are answered.
- Ordinary tasks wait for your reply. Active Goals wait up to ten minutes, then continue with recommended choices. Skipping, timeout and cancellation have distinct history labels. Questions do not count as completed work; after an answer, completed turns retain the usual no-progress checks.
- Waiting questions use the existing sidebar attention marker and notification setting. Keyboard controls work inside the panel, while double Escape outside it still stops the current task.
- Correct update download time estimates when the timer runs between progress reports, including the last few seconds of a transfer.

## 中文

- 新增“模型反问”：Pi 可一次提出 1–3 个问题，回答作为工具结果返回，同一回合继续执行，保留当前任务计划。
- 输入框上方的面板支持单选、多选、推荐项、其他回答和补充说明。切换会话保留未提交的答案；历史问答卡可展开、查找和复制。
- 输入框中的纯文字可直接回答第一题，其余问题按跳过处理。附有文件、图片、片段或引用时提示先完成回答，并保留全部草稿。
- 普通任务一直等待回复，执行中的 Goal 最多等待 10 分钟，超时按推荐项继续。跳过、超时和取消分别标记；等待回答时不结束当前回合，回答后仍按原有规则检查是否有实际进展。
- 待回答问题沿用侧栏提示和系统通知设置。面板内可用键盘作答，面板外连按两次 Esc 仍停止当前任务。
- 修正下载更新时计时器与进度上报错开造成的剩余时间偏大，补充下载最后几秒的验证。
