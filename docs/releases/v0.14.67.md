# Pi Desktop v0.14.67

- Clear the composer as soon as you press Enter. The message appears at the end of the chat marked "发送中" until Pi accepts it; during a run it waits in the queue instead. Sent text no longer shows in the composer and the chat at the same time, which could last for seconds or a whole turn.
- If a message cannot be sent, put its text, attachments, images, pasted snippets and quotes back into the composer. Anything typed in the meantime is kept, and the notice offers to restore the unsent text in front of it.
- Show the save result and copy confirmation on the button that was clicked instead of a separate notice. Notifications appear at the top right of the conversation, stay clear of the composer and approval cards, fade out when they close, and show at most three at a time. Repeating the same notice replays it instead of stacking a copy.
- Redesign the update dialog: the title names the target version, a downloaded update says it applies after a restart, "稍后" and "立即重启" sit together, and the automatic restart option has a short explanation.
- While a task runs, choose between adding to the current turn and running after it with two icon buttons next to the stop button. The send button always shows its arrow.

Validation: [composer clearing on send](https://github.com/yeyuProject/pi-desktop-gui/blob/v0.14.67/docs/testing/send-latency.md) and [feedback motion](https://github.com/yeyuProject/pi-desktop-gui/blob/v0.14.67/docs/testing/feedback-motion.md).

## 中文

- 按回车后输入框立即清空。消息先显示在对话末尾，标为「发送中」，Pi 接收后换成正式消息；任务运行中发送的消息先显示在队列里。不再出现刚发的文字同时留在输入框和对话里的情况，以前可能持续几秒，甚至整轮回复。
- 消息没发出去时，正文、附件、图片、粘贴片段和引用都会回到输入框。这期间输入的新内容会保留，提示里可以把没发出去的内容恢复到前面。
- 保存设置、复制等操作的结果直接显示在所点的按钮上，不再另外弹出提示。通知显示在对话区右上角，避开输入框和审批卡，关闭时淡出，同时最多显示 3 条；同一句提示再次出现时重新显示，不会叠出两条。
- 更新弹窗改版：标题写明要更新到的版本；下载完成后说明重启后生效；「稍后」和「立即重启」放在一起；「任务结束后自动重启」附简短说明。
- 任务运行中，停止按钮旁用两个图标按钮选择「立即补充」或「完成后执行」，发送按钮始终显示箭头图标。

验证记录见[发送时清空输入框](https://github.com/yeyuProject/pi-desktop-gui/blob/v0.14.67/docs/testing/send-latency.md)和[操作反馈动效](https://github.com/yeyuProject/pi-desktop-gui/blob/v0.14.67/docs/testing/feedback-motion.md)。
